## Trust model and data flow

Hosted and BYOC retain customer-local execution/content and stable workspace identity. Customer-authored source, plans/work items, prompts, transcripts, model I/O and diagnostic content are customer-side data. The vendor control plane receives allowlisted control metadata and commercial facts only: organization/workspace identifiers, versioned entitlement and price references, counters, provider bill amounts, operation/receipt identifiers and resource disposition.

The customer runtime must remain usable during vendor outage. No model call may require an online vendor-control metadata round trip. Provider/model requests go from the authorized customer runtime to OpenRouter/upstream providers; those are declared third parties and must meet the selected retention policy. BYOK customers control their provider account and keys; managed-key custody requires explicit service scope and limits. Do not centralize raw prompts to simplify invoices.

Transport endpoint identity, generation/revocation, browser-code delivery and key recovery are part of the promise. Vendor-served JavaScript with TLS/base64, vendor-root VM access or storage residency alone cannot establish vendor-unreadability. Reuse the sealed-channel/content-marker and outage checks; disclose the real hosted trust boundary.

BYOC grants are scoped, auditable and revocable. The customer pays its cloud directly; the separate management invoice covers Papercusp operations. A budget cannot cap unrelated spending in the customer's cloud account. Hosted caps apply to admitted Papercusp-managed exposure and explicitly disclosed retained-resource liability.
