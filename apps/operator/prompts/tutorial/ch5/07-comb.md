## Brief

**Cupboard** is the layer that lets separate Papercusp installations share what
they've learned — packaged improvements (prompts, configs, patterns) that
one Pot proves out and another can adopt. Think of it as the export/import
format for institutional knowledge, reviewed on the way in like any other
change.

## Details

The unit of exchange is a knowledge pack: a self-contained bundle describing
a proven improvement with its evidence. A Pot that develops a better
review-prompt or a sharper triage rubric can contribute it upstream; other
Pots browse, inspect, and install — nothing applies silently, and installs
are versioned and reversible.

This matters most in multi-machine and team setups (several Pots, one
organization) and for the broader ecosystem: fixes and know-how discovered
in one deployment don't have to be re-learned in every other.

Honest labeling: the Cupboard's plumbing exists and moves packs today, but it's one
of the younger subsystems — its documentation is still thin and its
workflows are actively evolving. Treat this section as orientation, not a
manual.
