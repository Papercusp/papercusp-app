Where the height was going

Three of the six rows restate something the card, its column, or the 2 px track already says: the slug is the title in kebab-case, the harness reads papercusp on every card, and n/m items is the track drawn in words.

Removing them is not a trade. The trade is the rail.

Both columns are pinned to 300px, which is roughly what minmax(190px, 1fr) resolves to on a wide window. Heights are measured live, so the counts change when you switch to worst-case content.

The four proposals

Each specimen is one live card at true column width. Heights are measured in your browser on render, not asserted.

At the narrowest the grid ever goes

.hud__cols is repeat(5, minmax(190px, 1fr)). Subtract the column border and padding, then the card's own border, padding, rail and gap, and the text has 146 px to live in. That is why each variant's rail carries one signal plus the exception pips rather than the whole meta strip relocated — and it is the real argument between B and C. Watch C here: at the floor its avatar stack runs into the card edge, which is the price of spending the rail on presence.

What changes, for the recommended B

Two files. No data-layer change — every value on the compact card is already on HudEntityCard.

ElementCarriesActionWhy
.hud__card-h dot + plan slug rebuild Becomes dot + title + right rail. The slug moves to the title attribute — it is the title in kebab-case plus a date, so the card was printing its name twice.
.hud__reason plan title, 2-line clamp promote Retired for plan cards in favour of .hud__cardtitle on line 1: 11.5px/600, full --fg, single line. Session cards keep .hud__reason untouched.
.hud__entity-why who, else next action keep This is the “now item”. Unchanged — it is already a single clamped line.
.hud__plan card.context = harness slug delete The unuseful third line. It reads papercusp on every card in a single-harness workspace, so it distinguishes nothing.
.hud__chips wip / blocked / todo / needs-you / archived triage wip and todo restate the progress track and the column the card is sitting in. Only the two exception counts survive, as .hud__pip dots in the right rail.
.hud__cardmeta ✎ ⚒ · n/m items · avatars · live pill float ⚒ and the live pill move into .hud__cardright on line 1. n/m items goes — the 2px track already draws it. ✎ and the avatar stack go too: measured at 244px, a full strip is 128px and leaves the title 69px. B spends the rail on activity; pick C instead if the stack matters more than the ages.
.hud__track settled / total keep Absolutely positioned, costs zero height, and is now the only place progress is stated.
.hud__cardright — add The right rail: margin-left:auto, flex:0 0 auto, so the title takes the remaining width and ellipsizes into it.
