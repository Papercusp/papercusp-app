Every zone named, every boundary drawn

Look 3 carried through to the top bar and the bottom panel, so all six regions of the popup are named, ruled zones rather than three framed ones floating among unlabelled chrome. The one decision worth arguing about is below: the horizontal zones are titled without spending a single row of height.

The design decision

Horizontal zones get an inline cap, not a header row

A rail is tall and narrow, so a full-width title bar across the top of it costs nothing you care about. The top bar and the two bottom bands are the opposite shape — short and wide — and giving each of them its own title row would add three rows to a popup we just spent this session compacting from five rows down to two. That would be undoing one of your asks to satisfy another.

So a horizontal zone is named by a small filled cap that sits inside the band's existing row, at its leading edge, in the same accent-on-ink as the rails' titles. Same vocabulary, same colour, zero added height. The FLEET label already worked this way — it just becomes a filled cap instead of grey text, and the pattern generalises to the other two.

Net height change: zero. The top bar and both bottom bands keep exactly the row count they have now. The only new pixels in the whole design are the two rail title bars, which sit in columns that were already tall.
