Session popup · placement study

Where does a question that’s waiting on you sit inside the conversation?

You picked D — questions become decision-tier cards in Needs you, plus a counter in the chrome, no new tab. Clicking one opens this popup. So the popup is where the answering actually happens, and it needs somewhere to put the card. Four placements below, drawn in the popup’s own chrome at its real type sizes.

Before designing — what is already built

The popup already has this region. It just filters your questions out.

SessionChatModal.tsx:1668 renders a pending-cards region between the status band and the transcript — data-testid="session-chat-pending-escalations", capped at 45% height, scrollable, one OtherDetail card per item, fed by the same plans.attention feed the board reads.

Its filter is the whole problem. Line 929: i.kind === 'coord-escalation' && isEscalationWithOptions(i). Both questions that sat unanswered are kind: 'conversation', so they are excluded twice over — wrong kind, and no ref.options to expand into buttons.

Nothing server-side drops them: useInboxAttention flattens the entire feed, and the answer path already exists (answerAttentionItem → resolveConversationAnswer, plus replyToAttentionItem, which delivers the reply to a live asker’s coord inbox and wakes them). This is a repair, not a new surface — which is why the options below argue about placement, not about whether to build a renderer.

What changes
The head question docks onto the composer and takes it over: preset options become buttons, the placeholder becomes Answer in your own words, and Send becomes Answer. A 2 for you cap in the status band carries the count; a queue line carries the rest.
Why it works
The question and the thing you answer it with become one object, at the one place your cursor already is. It never scrolls away, it costs the transcript nothing above the fold, and free text is a first-class answer rather than a fallback — which matters, because most of these are not really multiple-choice.
Honest cost
It re-points the composer, and the composer means something already. You must always be able to just say a thing — so this needs a visible way out (Just send a message) and it must never swallow a message you meant as a message. That is the one real risk on this page.

What changes
A Waiting on you section takes the top of the Activity rail, above Plan and Health, with the zone title in the warn variant.
Why it works
It costs the transcript nothing at all, and it inherits the rail’s existing section vocabulary — collapse state, sticky caps, scrolling — for free.
Honest cost
Two problems, and I would not ship it. The Activity rail is the did zone — a pending question is not history, so it fights the popup’s own reading order of told → talking → did. And the rail is the first thing that collapses at narrow widths, so the item most likely to be missed lands in the column most likely to be hidden. Included because it is the obvious suggestion, and it is worth writing down why it is wrong.

Recommendation

C, with B’s anchor as a link — and A as the fallback when more than one is open

The thing these questions need is not a bigger card, it is to be adjacent to the act of answering. Docking to the composer gets that, and it is the only option where a typed answer is the primary path instead of an escape hatch — which matters, because both real examples are judgement calls, not menus. Keep B’s see where this was asked as a link rather than a placement: it buys the reasoning without paying the anchoring cost or the scroll-away risk.

  1. Widen the filter first, and ship only that. Include kind: 'conversation' and option-less escalations in the popup’s pending set. The existing region renders them today — one predicate, and both of mine appear.
  2. Then move the head card to the composer dock and re-point the composer to replyToAttentionItem, which already delivers to a live asker and wakes them. Keep a plain Just send a message out, always visible.
  3. Overflow stays in the pinned band — one docked, the rest collapsed above the transcript with the Needs you cap. Nothing new; A becomes the queue rather than the answer.
  4. Skip D. Right rail is the wrong zone for a pending thing, and it is the first column to disappear.
