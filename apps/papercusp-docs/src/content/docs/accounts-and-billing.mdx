You can read this manual without signing in. Acting on a hosted workspace
requires the correct Papercusp account and organization access. An external
integration connection and a paid subscription are separate from sign-in.

## Create an account once

Choose **Create account**, verify the identity offered by the sign-up flow,
review the terms and privacy notice, and return to the portal.
For an existing identity, choose **Sign in** using the same provider.
If account creation says the identity is already registered, repeated
sign-ups will not repair access.

Use the account and organization that own the intended workspace. Being
signed in does not grant access to every project or another organization's
machines.

## Connect integrations separately

Gmail, Calendar and other integrations request their own authorization.
They do not create a Papercusp owner session, and disconnecting one should
not be confused with signing out of Papercusp.

Review provider permissions before connecting. Never include verification
links, session cookies, passwords or tokens in a support report.
See [Plugins](/docs/plugins) and [Remote access](/docs/remote-access).

## Use the billing page shown for your account

If your installation offers hosted subscriptions, open **Billing** and read
the current plans, status and restrictions. Use the plan and price displayed
there; this guide does not promise a fixed price, allowance or provider quota.
Local installations may show a different, read-only billing state.

Choose the displayed subscription action to open checkout.
After returning, wait for the portal's subscription confirmation.
The checkout return page alone is not proof that payment and entitlements
have been confirmed.

If **Manage billing** is available, it opens the supported billing-management
flow. Review its effective dates and cancellation consequences before making
changes. Billing controls can depend on your role in the organization.

## Payment pending or an unexpected restriction

Read the status message rather than starting duplicate subscriptions.
Record the time, the non-secret reference code and what the portal currently
reports. If confirmation or access remains wrong, use Support.

A subscription does not prove that cloud provisioning succeeded, and an
active plan does not imply unlimited resources. Check the workspace's status
and the limits actually shown by your installation.
See [Cloud workspaces](/docs/cloud-workspaces) and
[Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).
