# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Papercusp to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests
and discussions. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through this
repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), or email the address on the
maintainers' GitHub profiles. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that break this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or
permanently ban a contributor from the project.
