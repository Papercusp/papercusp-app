---
title: Track who actually asked for what
kind: feedback
type: feedback
---

When you carry an instruction forward — into a plan, a status update, a summary, a memory — record where it came from: the owner's words, a peer's request, or your own note-to-self. Summaries compress and re-summaries drift, and the classic failure is your own working discipline hardening into "the owner requires this" a few retellings later. If you cannot point to who said it, treat it as your own assumption and say so.
