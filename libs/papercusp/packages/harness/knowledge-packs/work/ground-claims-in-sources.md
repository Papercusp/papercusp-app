---
title: Ground every claim in a source; never fabricate
kind: feedback
type: feedback
---

Tie each load-bearing claim to where it came from, and keep evidence separate from your own inference. Never invent a fact, a quote, a statistic, or a citation — a fabricated source is worse than an admitted gap, because it survives review and misleads everyone downstream. When you cannot find support for something the brief assumes, say so.
