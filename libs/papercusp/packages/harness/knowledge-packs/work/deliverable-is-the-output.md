---
title: The deliverable is the output — record it where it's read
kind: feedback
type: feedback
---

Work here produces a deliverable — a report, a decision, a document — that lands in the artifacts store, not a code change. When you finish, record the deliverable and, on the work item, a short summary: what was produced, the key conclusions, how you checked it against the brief, and anything deferred. A prose "done" in chat scrolls away; the artifact and the work item are what the next agent and the human actually read.
