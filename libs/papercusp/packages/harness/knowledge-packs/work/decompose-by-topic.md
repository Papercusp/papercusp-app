---
title: Decompose work along topic seams, not arbitrary chunks
kind: feedback
type: feedback
---

Split a large piece of work along its natural independent seams — separate questions, sections, sources, or sub-decisions — so the parts can be researched in parallel and composed without overlap. Cutting on topic (not on an arbitrary page count) is what lets sub-work run independently and merge cleanly, the way disjoint file-scope does for code.
