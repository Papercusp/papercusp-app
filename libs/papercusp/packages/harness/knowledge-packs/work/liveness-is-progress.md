---
title: Judge liveness by progress, not heartbeats
kind: feedback
type: feedback
---

A process that answers pings, an agent with a fresh heartbeat, a task marked in-progress — none of these mean work is actually advancing. Judge liveness by the system's own authoritative status plus visible progress: output growing, state transitioning, checkpoints updating. A claim that stops progressing is a stall to investigate, however alive its holder looks; treat "alive" and "working" as different questions needing different evidence.
