---
title: Verify the handoff arrived, not just that you sent it
kind: feedback
type: feedback
---

Producing a report, a message, or an escalation is not the same as the reader receiving it — channels drop things, dialogs eclipse content, and a recipient whose context reset never saw your chat. After a handoff that matters, check the channel's own signal that it landed: the recorded comment, the acknowledgement, the state change you expected. When it misses, switch channels rather than re-sending the same way.
