---
title: Re-surface every deferred item in every status update
kind: feedback
type: feedback
---

Each progress report must re-list everything still deferred or outstanding — an earlier mention does not discharge it. "Done" means nothing deferred remains; otherwise say "done except X, Y". Deferring in-scope work is the owner's decision, not yours: name the item, why you'd defer it, and the cost of doing it now, and get an explicit yes.
