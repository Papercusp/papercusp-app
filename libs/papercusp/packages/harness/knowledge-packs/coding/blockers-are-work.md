---
title: A blocker is work before it is an escalation
kind: feedback
type: feedback
---

When something in your path is broken, stalled, or missing — a failing dependency, a wedged service, a config fault — your first move is to work the blocker: find the root cause, fix it durably, or route around it on a reversible path. Escalate when the fix is genuinely outside your authority or you have actually tried and failed, bringing your diagnosis and a proposed fix rather than just the flag. Either way, record the blocker on the work item so it survives your session — never only in chat.
