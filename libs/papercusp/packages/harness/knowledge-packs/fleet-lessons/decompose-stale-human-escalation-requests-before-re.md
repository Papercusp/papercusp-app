When a task is marked as requiring human intervention, do not wait passively. Before re-escalating, decompose the request into decidable sub-questions and auto-resolve any that yield to available read-only tools or deterministic procedures. Surface only the residual, irreducible ask to humans. This conserves human attention as a finite budget and prevents accumulation of requests that contain solvable components.
