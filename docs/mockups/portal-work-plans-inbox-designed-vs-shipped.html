Closing the gap

Two honest routes. The first keeps everything that shipped and adds the missing pane where the page is already rendered.

Route 1 · Recommended — add the detail pane to the operator's full-page routes

Keep the iframe embed. When PlansPane / InboxPane render inside .portal-focused-work-surface, lay them out as list + detail; the sidebar keeps its single column untouched.

  • Plans: list left (existing rows, filters, clean-up strip); the plan dashboard on the right instead of taking over the page. Selection is URL-backed (?plan=<slug>, nuqs).
  • Inbox: list left (existing rows, bulk strip); OtherDetail + discussion on the right instead of expanding inline. Selection is URL-backed (?item=<id>).
  • Portal deep links forward plan / item into the iframe URL so the board's link shapes work end to end.
  • Under ~900 px the detail becomes a route-backed screen — the board's narrow state.
Route 2 — native portal surfaces

Rebuild Plans and Inbox inside the Next portal against the operator's APIs, straight from the board's IR spec. Cleanest deep links and theming, but it duplicates readers and the bulk flows the embed carried over for free — and the board's own recommendation leaned on composition.
