Placement rule

The auction exists only inside the current-offer slot. Nothing is inserted below the video, so the stream, chat tabs, and surrounding page keep their positions.

Why this direction

Continuity beats duplication.

The surface reads as one product moving through a sale—not two competing offers that happen to reference the same item.

01
Preserve the known starting point

No active auction means the current Now Selling card remains familiar, complete, and actionable.

02
Transform in place

When bidding opens, urgency, price, activity, leader, and input replace the card contents inside the same bounded slot.

03
Make closure explicit

Settling distinguishes “bidding ended” from “winner confirmed,” and Sold preserves the authoritative outcome.
