One place to resolve everything blocked on you

Three interface directions for plans, work items, owner walls, and agent sessions. The differentiator is not the list—it is a shared resolver that records the answer, clears linked gates, and resumes waiting work.

Recommendation: Option A Build the Resolution Inbox first, then offer Option C as a focus mode. Reserve Option B for genuinely high-volume operations.

Option A Recommended

Resolution Inbox

A calm master-detail queue: see the full blocker set, then resolve the selected case without losing context.

Best at

  • Balancing queue visibility with fast resolution
  • Explaining impact before an irreversible answer
  • Becoming the canonical deep-link destination

Tradeoff: needs a robust resolver contract before every source feels equally actionable.

Option B

Blocker Workbench

A dense three-column operations surface for sorting, inspecting, and resolving large blocker populations.

Best at

  • High-volume triage and keyboard workflows
  • Comparing age, impact, source, and liveness
  • Batching only resolution-compatible cases

Tradeoff: heavier cognitive load and greater risk of treating unlike blockers as bulk rows.

Option C

Unblock Focus

A guided, one-at-a-time flow that makes each decision legible and advances automatically after resolution.

Best at

  • Reducing decision fatigue and visual noise
  • Clearing a queue sequentially
  • Touch, narrow-window, and presentation use

Tradeoff: weak global awareness if offered without the full Inbox behind it.
